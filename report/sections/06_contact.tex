\section{Unilateral Angular Contact}
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{llll}\ParamHeader
$\theta_w$ & Wall angle & $\pi/2$ rad & Test setting\\
$K_e$ & Angular stiffness & $0.5$ N m/rad & Exploratory\\
$\zeta_e$ & Contact damping scale & $0.7$ & Heuristic\\
$D_e$ & Angular damping & $2\zeta_e\sqrt{JK_e}$ & Calculated\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
\[
\delta=\theta_l-\theta_w,\qquad
r_e=\begin{cases}0&\delta\leq0\\
\max(0,K_e\delta+D_e\omega_l)&\delta>0
\end{cases},\qquad \tau_e=-r_e
\]
\textbf{Connection:} Subtract $r_e$ at the load-side torque sum.
The wall neither acts before contact nor pulls during separation.
\textbf{Scope:} Angular virtual wall, not planar geometric contact.
The damping estimate excludes existing viscous friction and active control.
\textbf{First test:} Single target change from $\theta_w-0.05$ to $\theta_w+0.05$ rad.
