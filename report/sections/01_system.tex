\textbf{Qualification:} Steady forward-drive estimate only. Motor thermal limits, operating efficiency and assembly variant remain to be verified.
The ideal baseline uses $\eta=1$; catalog efficiency is not a friction model.
\[
J_{m\rightarrow l}=N^2J_m\simeq9.423\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm{kg\,m^2}

\textbf{Haptic implication:} Reflected rotor inertia increases. Backdrivability requires separate assessment.
